Wireshark lab: reading a pcap file

Study Guide

The subnetting field manual

A complete, self-paced subnetting guide for Modules 11 and 12. It covers binary, masks and ANDing, the block-size method, borrowing bits, VLSM, and IPv6 subnet IDs, with a step-by-step calculator and practice drills that generate new problems every time.

GUIDE

Subnetting Field Manual

Learning path, analogies for every concept, a cheat sheet, 30+ worked examples in every octet, the class "7 holy steps" method, a visual subnet slicer, a calculator that shows its work, and eight practice drills (including VLSM and IPv6), plus flashcards, a quiz, and a glossary.
